# SahayaSetu - Disaster Recovery Skill-Matching & Rehabilitation Platform
## Product Requirements Document (PRD-DRSM-001)

### Key Architectural & Functional Directives
1. **Multi-Tenancy & Strict Region Scoping (RBAC)**: Regional NGO Admin can only view and manage within their assigned `region_id`. Super Admins retain statewide/cross-regional oversight.
2. **Core Workflows**:
   - **Beneficiary Profile Intake (BPI)**: Rapid field registration with calamity tags, vocational skills, living/camp status, contact info.
   - **Job Requisitions & Skill-Matching Engine (JME)**: Emergency contractor/NGO jobs matched against available vocational talent in the district with one-click candidate assignment.
   - **Super Admin Global Analytics (SGA)**: Cross-region metrics, district comparative tables, skill shortage indices, and region provisioning.
   - **Beneficiary Self-Portal / Placement View**: For affected users to verify daily wages, active reconstruction assignments, and emergency notifications.
